1. Purpose and scope — natural hazards only

MEW is a volunteer-run, non-commercial community information project for resident observations of natural disasters, severe weather, and environmental hazards in the Melbourne/Brevard County area. Permitted topics include flooding, storm damage, fallen trees, tornado damage, visible smoke or wildfire observations, debris, road washouts, coastal flooding, erosion, and utility disruptions. Crime reports, suspicious-person reports, missing-person reports, medical emergencies, personal disputes, and accusations about individuals are not permitted and may be removed.

2. Volunteer project — not an emergency service

Melbourne Emergency Watch (“MEW”) is intended to be a volunteer-run, non-commercial community information project. It is not a government agency, public safety authority, emergency dispatcher, 911 service, professional monitoring service, or official source of emergency instructions. It does not dispatch responders, guarantee that anyone is monitoring reports, or promise that a report will be reviewed or acted upon.

3. No guarantee; information must be independently verified

Reports, map pins, photographs, comments, status labels, and other information may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, misleading, duplicated, malicious, or unavailable. “Confirmed” means only that a MEW volunteer or authorized user reviewed the submission under the website’s process; it is not an official finding or a guarantee of safety, accuracy, or current conditions. You must independently verify information with appropriate official sources before acting on it.

4. Emergencies and personal safety

Never rely on MEW to report an emergency or obtain emergency assistance. For immediate danger, call 911. Follow instructions from law enforcement, fire-rescue, emergency medical services, and official local, state, and federal authorities. Do not enter floodwater, approach fires, downed power lines, damaged structures, crime scenes, or other dangerous areas to take photographs or verify a report. Do not delay evacuation or protective action to use this website.
