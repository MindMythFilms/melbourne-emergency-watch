Photo upload rules — please read before selecting images
  • Only upload photos you personally took or have clear permission and legal rights to share. No copyrighted images taken from websites, social media, news, or other people.
  • No logos, trademarks, watermarks, or branded signage.
  • No people in photos at all. This is an absolute rule, even if a person is in the background, their face is hidden, blurred, or not recognizable. Do not upload images containing bystanders, pedestrians, victims, children, police officers, firefighters, EMTs, or any other person.
  • No license plates or other identifying vehicle details.
  • Do not show private information such as home addresses, documents, phone numbers, screens, or personal records.
  • No graphic, disturbing, sexual, threatening, misleading, or edited/manipulated images.
  • Photograph only from a safe, lawful location. Never approach danger or delay getting to safety to take a picture.

Photos that break these rules may be rejected or removed. All photos remain private until an administrator completes a content and privacy review. Approval does not verify the incident.

About CoastWatch FL

CoastWatch FL is a volunteer-run, non-commercial community information project. It is not affiliated with any government, police, fire, emergency management, or utility agency.

Reports are user-submitted and may be inaccurate, incomplete, delayed, or removed. New reports appear automatically as “Unverified community report.” CoastWatch FL does not fact-check or independently verify reports. Approved photographs have passed a content and privacy screening only; photo approval does not verify the reported event.

Do not approach hazards to take photographs. Never enter floodwater, approach fires or downed power lines, or enter damaged structures. For emergencies call 911. For official weather alerts visit National Weather Service and Florida Division of Emergency Management.
