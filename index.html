About MEW

Melbourne Emergency Watch is a volunteer-run, non-commercial community information project. It is not affiliated with any government, police, fire, emergency management, or utility agency.

Reports are user-submitted and may be inaccurate, incomplete, delayed, or removed. “Community-reviewed” means a MEW administrator reviewed a submission; it does not mean an official agency verified it.

Do not approach hazards to take photographs. Never enter floodwater, approach fires or downed power lines, or enter damaged structures. For emergencies call 911. For official weather alerts visit National Weather Service and Florida Division of Emergency Management.
