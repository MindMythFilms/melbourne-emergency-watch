CoastWatch FL · Official NWS Coastal Hazard · November 9, 2026

⚠️ High Rip Current Risk Along the Atlantic Coast

The National Weather Service Melbourne has issued a High Rip Current Risk for beaches in Volusia, Indian River, St. Lucie, Martin, and Brevard Counties.

In effect through late Tuesday night, November 10. Dangerous rip currents can pull even experienced swimmers away from shore into deeper water. Entering the surf is strongly discouraged.

Yellow map polygon: approximate Brevard coastal risk corridor

If caught in a rip current, stay calm and float. Do not swim against the current. If able, swim parallel to the shoreline; if you cannot escape, face shore and signal or call for help. Follow lifeguards, beach flags, and posted signs.

Read the official National Weather Service alert.

Source: National Weather Service Melbourne, issued 10:44 PM EST November 9, 2026. The yellow polygon is a visual approximation of the Brevard beach corridor, not a surveyed hazard boundary. The advisory itself covers the listed counties.

CoastWatch FL · Flood Safety Advisory · Nov 7, 2026

Floodwater is going down. The danger may not be.

As floodwaters recede in parts of Central and South Florida, standing water can still hide serious hazards. Water that looks shallow or harmless may be contaminated or conceal dangers you cannot see.

⚠️ Stay out of floodwater. Do not walk, swim, or drive through flooded areas—even after the rain stops and water levels begin to fall.
🦠 Contaminated waterSewage, bacteria, chemicals, and other pollutants can cause illness or infection.
⚠️ Hidden debrisFloodwater can conceal broken glass, nails, sharp objects, damaged pavement, and unstable ground.
🐍 WildlifeSnakes, fire ants, insects, and other displaced animals may be present in or near standing water.
⚡ Electrical hazardsDowned power lines can energize surrounding water. Keep away and report them immediately.

Never take a chance with floodwater. Turn around, find another route, and keep children and pets away from flooded areas.

Follow local emergency instructions and check official updates from the Florida Division of Emergency Management. CoastWatch FL is a community information platform—not an emergency response service. For immediate danger, call 911.

BREVARD COUNTY · FLOOD RECOVERY · NOV 5–6, 2026

Flood cleanup: protect yourself

Avoid floodwater. Wear waterproof boots, gloves and protective clothing during cleanup. Disinfect salvageable surfaces, wash hands often, and discard food, drinks or medicines exposed to floodwater. Keep clear of downed power lines and never drive through standing water.

Separate debris into vegetative, household and construction piles. Keep hazardous household waste out of curbside piles.

Watch for post-flood contractor scams

Before hiring cleanup or repair contractors, verify licenses, get written estimates, avoid full payment upfront, never sign blank contracts, and confirm required permits. Be cautious of unsolicited door-to-door offers and high-pressure demands.

Welcome to CoastWatch FL

A community project built by a neighbor, to help neighbors stay informed. Please take a moment to review these simple terms.

Thanks for being here. CoastWatch FL is a volunteer-run, non-commercial community information project. These terms explain what the site is, how to use it safely, and what information is involved.

1. A community tool — not an emergency service

CoastWatch FL is not a government agency, 911 service, emergency dispatcher, or professional monitoring service. Reports are not sent to emergency responders. No one is guaranteed to be watching the site or able to respond. For emergencies or immediate danger, call 911.

2. Community reports are not official verification

Reports, map pins, photographs, and status labels are submitted or reviewed by community members. They may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, duplicated, or unavailable. A label such as “reviewed” or “confirmed” means only that an authorized CoastWatch FL volunteer reviewed the submission; it is not an official finding or a guarantee that a location is safe. Check official sources before making decisions.

3. Please put safety first

Do not enter floodwater, approach fires or downed power lines, enter damaged buildings, or put yourself or anyone else at risk to make a report or take a photograph. Follow instructions from emergency personnel and official authorities. Do not delay evacuation or other protective action to use this website.

4. Use of the site and responsibility

You decide whether and how to use information on CoastWatch FL. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the project owner and its volunteers are not responsible for losses or harm arising from your use of the site, reliance on community-submitted information, service interruptions, or actions taken by other users. Nothing in these terms is intended to waive rights or limit responsibilities that cannot legally be waived or limited.

5. Community submissions

Please submit information honestly and only when it is relevant to community safety. Do not trespass, impersonate officials, threaten others, or submit unlawful, abusive, graphic, intimate, or identifying material. Avoid sharing private personal information. Only upload photographs you have the right and permission to share. You are responsible for the content you submit.

6. Moderation and availability

CoastWatch FL volunteers may review, approve, edit, hide, or remove submissions and may restrict access when needed to protect the community. Reports and photographs may not be reviewed immediately. The site may be unavailable or changed without notice.

7. No professional relationship or advice

Information on this site is not legal, medical, engineering, law-enforcement, weather, or emergency-management advice. Using the site does not create a professional, agency, employment, or other formal relationship with its owner or volunteers.

8. Privacy — what the site handles

CoastWatch FL does not require an account to browse or submit an anonymous report. When you submit a report, the information you enter may be stored, including map coordinates or address, description, category, optional display name, and photos you choose to upload. Report locations are shown on the public map.

Photos are uploaded for review and are not displayed publicly unless approved by a moderator. The site records acceptance of these terms, including the terms version, time of acceptance, a browser-session identifier, and browser/user-agent information. Website service providers may process technical connection information as part of hosting and security.

The optional “My location” button asks your device for its current location so the map can center on you. That location is used in the page and is not sent to CoastWatch FL or saved as a visitor-location record. You can select a location manually instead. You do not have to use this feature.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information about yourself or others. For privacy questions or removal requests, use the contact information on the website.

9. Your choice

By selecting “I agree — continue,” you confirm that you have read and agree to these terms. You are not required to provide your device location. If you do not agree, choose “I do not agree — leave website” and do not use the site.
